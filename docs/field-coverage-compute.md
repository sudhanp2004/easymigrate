# Compute generator field coverage

Generated by `scripts/gen_differences_doc.py` from `easymigrate.plugins.compute.generate_gcp.FIELD_COVERAGE`. Do not edit by hand.

Every IR field the compute generator could touch is declared here, so an IR field added without declaring how the generator handles it fails CI (no silent mapping).
